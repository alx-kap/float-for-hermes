Appearance

Built like a Mac app

Small on screen. Not on features.

Float is plain SwiftUI and AppKit with no third-party dependencies, so it's light, quick to open and feels like it came with your Mac.

Liquid Glass. A translucent panel that floats over your work and follows your light or dark appearance.

Lives in the menu bar. No Dock icon. Close the panel mid-answer and a notification tells you when the reply is ready.

Profiles and models. Switch between Hermes profiles and models right from the header.

Past chats on ⌘K. Search, rename, archive and pick up any old conversation where you left it.

You stay in charge. Approvals for risky commands, clarifying questions and secret prompts appear right in the chat.

Files in, files out. Drag, paste or ⌘O. Images and files Hermes sends back show inline, ready to save or open in Preview.

Retry, regenerate, edit. Redo the latest message any way you like. Messages typed offline send when you're back.

Context at a glance. A ring in the header shows how full the context window is, with a token breakdown one click away.

Slash commands. Type / for autocomplete, ↑↓ to pick, ⇥ to add arguments.

Voice, on device

Just say it.

Press ⇧⌘D and talk. Float listens, sends when you pause, reads the reply aloud and listens again. Speech is transcribed by Apple's on-device models, so your voice never leaves your Mac.

  • No API key. Nothing extra to set up or pay for.
  • Your voice of choice. Pick a Siri voice in Read & Speak and Float answers in it.
  • Just one message? ⌘D dictates a single message.

Questions.

Is this an official Hermes app?

No. Float is an independent, community-made client. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Nous Research.

What do I need?

A Mac on macOS 26 Tahoe or later, and a Hermes gateway you can reach, like the one the Hermes desktop app uses. Float finds it from the desktop app's settings. Tailscale works well for reaching it from anywhere.

Where does my data go?

Only to the gateway you point Float at. Sign-in happens in your browser on your gateway's own login page, and tokens are kept in the macOS Keychain.

Why does the installer clear the quarantine flag?

Float is signed but not notarized, because notarizing needs a paid Apple developer account. Clearing the flag skips the "can't verify this app" prompt. Read the script before you run it.

Get Float.

One line in Terminal puts the latest release in ~/Applications and opens it. Then look for the bolt in your menu bar.

$ curl -fsSL https://alx-kap.github.io/float-for-hermes/install.sh | bash
  • macOS 26 or later
  • A reachable Hermes gateway
  • Free, MIT licensed

Rather download it?

Get Float.zip, unzip it and drag Float into Applications. The first time, right-click it and choose Open.

Or build it yourself

Needs Xcode 26. The script installs XcodeGen if it's missing.

git clone https://github.com/alx-kap/float-for-hermes.git
cd float-for-hermes && ./install.sh
